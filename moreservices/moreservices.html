Rental Services

Renting on RentMate takes just a few steps. Sign up and get verified, search for the item you need, check when it is available, and send your request. Owners approve it, you track its status, and the return is confirmed. No guesswork, no hassle.

RENTAL SERVICES

SERVICE 01
Students and instructors can register an account to access the equipment rental system.
SERVICE 02
Administrators can verify that registered users are DOrSU students or instructors.
SERVICE 03
Owners can post their equipment for rent with its name, description, category, condition, photo, rental rate, and availability.
SERVICE 04
Users can view the equipment offered by other users.
SERVICE 05
Users can search for an item based on its name, category, or availability.
SERVICE 06
Users can check the dates when an item is available for rent.
SERVICE 07
Users can submit a request to rent a selected item for their chosen dates.
SERVICE 08
Owners can approve or decline rental requests based on their availability and other requirements.
SERVICE 09
Users can check the status of their requests, such as pending, approved, declined, ongoing, returned, cancelled, or completed.
SERVICE 10
Owners can confirm that an item has been returned and report if it is late or damaged.
