About Our Company

InnoByte is a team of BSIT students from Davao Oriental State University (DOrSU). We built RentMate, a web-based peer-to-peer equipment rental system for the DOrSU community. RentMate helps students and instructors rent and lend personal items like laptops, chargers, calculators, instruments, etc.. . Our goal is to make borrowing easier, cheaper, and more organized, so no one has to buy something they only need for a few days.

Mission

To give the DOrSU community a safe and convenient way to share and rent equipment.

Vision

A university where students and instructors can easily get what they need, when they need it, from each other.

Our History

InnoByte was formed in 2026 by four BSIT 2C students. We first planned a facility reservation system called RoomMate. After more discussion, we saw that many students and instructors struggle to find equipment for projects, performances, and classes. So we changed the idea to RentMate, a platform where members of the community can rent items from each other. From a class proposal, it grew into a working website that we continue to improve.
